# Warranty
categories: warranty, vehicle_fault

## [WAR-1] Standard new-car warranty
New vehicles carry a 5-year or 150,000 km warranty, whichever comes first, covering manufacturing defects in parts and labour. Wear items (tyres, brake pads, wiper blades) and damage from accidents or misuse are not covered.

## [WAR-2] Warranty decisions and appeals
If a claim is declined, the customer receives the reason in writing. The customer may appeal within 30 days; an appeal is reviewed by a senior technician who did not make the original decision, and the outcome is given within 10 working days.

## [WAR-3] Servicing outside the network
Servicing at an independent garage does not void the warranty, provided the service followed the manufacturer schedule and used approved parts. The customer may be asked for invoices.
