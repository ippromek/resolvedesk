# Personal data
categories: data_privacy

## [PRV-1] Data requests
Customers can ask for a copy of their personal data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by replying to any message from us. We confirm receipt within 2 working days and complete the request within 30 days. Records we must keep by law are retained.

## [PRV-2] Marketing consent
Customers can withdraw marketing consent at any time. Withdrawal takes effect within 3 working days across email, SMS and phone.
