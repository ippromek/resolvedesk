# Service experience
categories: service_experience

## [SER-1] Service standards
We aim to return serviced vehicles on the promised day. If a delay is expected, the service advisor must call the customer before the promised collection time.

## [SER-2] Staff conduct
Customers should be treated with courtesy at all times. Complaints about staff conduct are reviewed by the branch manager, who contacts the customer personally.

## [SER-3] Repeat repairs
If the same fault returns within 90 days of a repair, the follow-up repair labour is not charged.
