# Sales and delivery
categories: sales, delivery_delay

## [SAL-1] Delivery dates
Delivery dates are estimates given at order. If a delivery is delayed by more than 14 days beyond the estimate, the customer may cancel the order and receive the deposit back in full, or keep the order and receive a monthly update from their sales consultant.

## [SAL-2] Sales information
Prices, specifications and finance terms quoted in writing by a sales consultant are honoured for the validity period stated on the quotation. Complaints about misleading information are reviewed by the sales manager.

## [SAL-3] Trade-in valuations
Trade-in valuations are valid for 7 days and subject to a final inspection on handover.
