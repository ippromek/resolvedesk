# Vehicle safety concerns
categories: vehicle_fault

## [SAF-1] Possible safety defects
If a customer reports a symptom that could affect safety (brakes, steering, airbags, loss of power, smoke or fire), we advise them not to drive the vehicle. Our 24/7 roadside assistance will collect the vehicle free of charge from anywhere in the UAE. Safety cases are inspected within one working day of the vehicle reaching the workshop.

## [SAF-2] Courtesy car during safety inspections
While a vehicle is held for a safety inspection or repair, the customer is offered a courtesy car of a similar class, subject to availability and a valid driving licence.

## [SAF-3] Manufacturer recalls
If the inspection links the fault to an open manufacturer recall, the recall repair is carried out at no cost to the customer and we inform the manufacturer.
