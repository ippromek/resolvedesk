# Billing and refunds
categories: billing

## [BIL-1] Invoice disputes
A customer who believes an invoice is wrong can dispute it within 60 days. We review the invoice line by line against the approved job card and reply within 5 working days.

## [BIL-2] Refunds
Where an overcharge is confirmed, we refund the difference to the original payment method within 14 days of confirmation. Work that the customer did not approve on the job card is not chargeable.

## [BIL-3] Estimates
Any additional work above the written estimate must be approved by the customer before it is carried out.
